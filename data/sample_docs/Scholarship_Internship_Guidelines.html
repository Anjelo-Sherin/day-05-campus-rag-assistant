Financial Aid, Scholarships & Industrial Internship Guidelines (2026)

Section 1: Merit-cum-Means (MCM) and Dean's Merit Scholarship

The Merit-cum-Means (MCM) Scholarship provides a 50% tuition fee waiver plus a monthly stipend of Rs. 3,000 for ten months each academic year. To qualify for the MCM Scholarship, a student must have an annual gross family income below Rs. 6,00,000 (Six Lakhs), maintain a minimum CGPA of 7.50, and have no active disciplinary or academic backlog records. Applications close on September 30 every year.

The Dean's Merit Scholarship is awarded automatically to the top 5% of students in each branch based on yearly CGPA (minimum 9.00 CGPA required) and provides a one-time cash award of Rs. 40,000.

Section 2: Semester-Long Industrial Internship & NOC Rules

Final-year (7th or 8th semester) B.Tech students with a CGPA of 7.00 or above and zero pending backlogs are eligible to pursue a 6-month (24-week) external industrial or research internship equivalent to 12 academic credits. Students must submit an official offer letter to the Career Development Cell (CDC) at least 15 days before the internship start date to obtain a No Objection Certificate (NOC).

Evaluation of the 6-month internship requires a Mid-Term Progress Review at Week 10 (30% weight) and a Final Technical Report + Joint Industry-Faculty Viva at Week 24 (70% weight).
